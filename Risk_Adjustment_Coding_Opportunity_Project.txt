Risk Adjustment Coding Opportunity Identification Platform

Business Problem US Medicare Advantage insurers are reimbursed based on
members’ documented risk profile. Chronic conditions are captured
through ICD-10 diagnosis codes which map to CMS Hierarchical Condition
Categories (HCCs). When a chronic condition is clinically present but
not documented on a claim, the payer loses appropriate risk adjustment
revenue. The objective is to identify encounters with potential missing
HCC diagnoses for coder review.

Population - Medicare Advantage members - 24 months historical claims
and clinical data - Prediction at encounter level - Human coder
validates all recommendations

Data Sources 1. Medical Claims: Claim ID, Member ID, Provider ID,
ICD-10, CPT, DOS, POS, Paid Amount, Allowed Amount. 2. EHR Notes: SOAP
notes, assessment, plan, physician notes. 3. Problem List: Persistent
chronic conditions. 4. Lab Results: HbA1c, eGFR, Creatinine, BNP, LDL
etc. 5. Medication History: Drug classes indicating chronic disease. 6.
Historical Diagnoses: Prior ICD-10 and HCC history. 7. Provider Master:
Specialty, organization, audit history, coding accuracy. 8. Member
Profile: Demographics, RAF, enrollment, utilization.

Feature Engineering - Previous HCC count - RAF score - Diagnosis
persistence - Medication-diagnosis consistency - Lab abnormality
indicators - Provider coding history - Specialty visit counts - ER,
inpatient and outpatient utilization - Procedure-diagnosis consistency -
Chronic disease burden - Claim frequency

Target Label Target = 1 if retrospective review or subsequent validated
encounter confirms an HCC diagnosis opportunity that was missing in the
current encounter; otherwise 0.

Model CatBoost/XGBoost binary classifier. Output: - Missing HCC
probability - Risk category

Explainability SHAP identifies drivers such as: - Prior CHF diagnosis -
Elevated BNP - Lasix prescription - Cardiology visit - Historical HCC

Post Model Analysis - Provider-wise coding opportunity rate -
Specialty-wise trends - Region-wise trends - Financial impact
estimation - HCC-wise opportunity analysis - False positive review -
Drift monitoring - Fairness monitoring

AI Layer The LLM never predicts diagnoses. It consumes: - Model
probability - SHAP values - Clinical notes - Lab summaries - Medication
history

It generates: - Coder-ready clinical summary - Evidence supporting
suspected missing HCC - Supporting citations from patient history -
Suggested review rationale

Business Outcome Improves coding completeness, supports accurate
Medicare Advantage risk adjustment, prioritizes coder workload, and
provides transparent evidence while keeping final coding decisions with
certified medical coders.
